1. What SevaGo is

SevaGo is a marketplace. A person who needs something done (a “Poster”) describes a task, and a verified person nearby (a “Partner”) accepts it. SevaGo does not perform tasks. Partners are independent service providers, not employees, agents or partners of SevaGo, and they decide how a task is carried out. SevaGo provides the app, matching, messaging, calling, payment protection and support.

2. Who can use it

To register you must be at least 18, be a resident of India, and have the legal capacity to make a binding contract under the Indian Contract Act, 1872. If you register for a business, you must have authority to bind it. Partners must also complete identity verification before they can take work.

3. Your account

Give accurate information and keep it up to date. You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code. You are responsible for what happens under your account, so keep your phone secure and tell us at once at team@sevago.co.in if you think someone else has used it. One person has one account; the same number can be both a Poster and a Partner.

4. Posters

  • Post lawful, clearly described tasks, with the real address and a fair budget. Tasks that are unlawful, unsafe, abusive or sexual in nature are not allowed, and wording that looks like that is flagged for review.
  • The price you see before you pay is the price you agree to. Pay only inside SevaGo.
  • When the Partner says the work is finished, check it and approve it. If you do not respond, the task is approved automatically after the period shown in the app.
  • If something is wrong, raise it before you approve the work (see section 6).

5. Partners

  • Accept only tasks you can do. Accepting is a commitment; repeated cancellations can limit your account.
  • Describe your skills and identity honestly. Verification documents must be genuine and yours.
  • You are responsible for your own taxes, permits and insurance, and for the way you carry out the work.
  • You are paid the task price less the SevaGo fee shown to you, after the Poster approves the work, to the payout account you registered. Payouts follow the withdrawal rules shown in the app, which can change, and can be paused.

6. Payments, fees and refunds

How payment works. Posters pay through the app, using our payment provider. SevaGo does not see or store card or UPI credentials. When you pay for a task, your payment is protected until you approve the work, and then it is released to the Partner.

Fees. SevaGo charges a platform fee and a payment protection fee to the Poster, and a fee to the Partner that is taken from the task price. The fees that apply are shown in the app before you pay or accept; we may change them for future tasks.

Cancelling and refunds. What you get back if you cancel depends on the stage the task has reached and is shown in the app before you confirm the cancellation. Refunds are made to the way you paid. For a refund after the work has started, SevaGo decides case by case. Once the Partner has been paid for completed work, SevaGo does not refund the payment. If the work was not done properly, raise a dispute before you approve it.

Off-app payments. Paying or asking to be paid outside SevaGo is not allowed. A payment made outside SevaGo is not protected and cannot be refunded or disputed through SevaGo.

Taxes. Each user is responsible for the taxes that apply to what they earn or pay through SevaGo.

8. Chat and calls

Chat and calls are for the task. Messages are checked automatically for phone numbers, email addresses, payment IDs and links, which are hidden, and for talk of paying outside SevaGo, which we flag; flagged messages may be reviewed by our team. Calls go through SevaGo and SevaGo does not record them. Be polite on calls: abuse or harassment can be reported and may lead to suspension.

9. Safety

In an emergency, call the police or the national emergency number first. Report unsafe behaviour to us at team@sevago.co.in.
