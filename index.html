What exactly is SevaGo?
+
SevaGo is India's first get-anything-done marketplace. Unlike category-based platforms, you can post literally any task, no matter how unusual and someone near you will take it on. You set the budget, choose who does it, and pay only when it's done.
How does payment work?
+
Payment is held securely in the app until the task is complete to your satisfaction. Once you confirm, the tasker gets paid instantly. No cash, no awkward conversations just clean, secure, in-app transactions only.
Are Taskers verified?
+
Yes. Every tasker goes through ID verification and builds their reputation through ratings and reviews. You can see their task history, star rating, and reviews before choosing who to assign your task to.
How do I earn as a Tasker?
+
Download the app, create your profile, and start browsing tasks nearby. No joining fee, no monthly charges. Pick tasks that fit your skills or schedule, send your offer, and earn when you complete them.
Where is SevaGo available?
+
Currently live in Bengaluru, with Coimbatore, Chennai and Kochi coming soon — South India first. We're expanding fast so download the app and be among the first when we launch in your city.
What kinds of tasks can I post?
+
Anything legal. From picking up dry cleaning to assembling furniture to queuing for a product launch. If you don't feel like doing it yourself, post it on SevaGo.
Is it safe to let a stranger do my task?
+
Absolutely. Every tasker is ID-verified, rated by past task posters, and reviewed publicly. You can see their full history before accepting anyone. And payment never leaves your account until you confirm the task is done.
Am I a User or a Tasker? Do I need two accounts?
+
You only need one account for both. Sign up as a User and you can post tasks. But if you spot a task you want to complete, just send an offer and earn from it too. Signed up as a Tasker first? You can post your own tasks anytime as well. One account, both sides, zero hassle.
How do I reach the SevaGo team?
+
For general queries, support, press, or investor enquiries email us at hello@sevago.co.in. We read every message and respond personally. You can also find us on Instagram and LinkedIn.
