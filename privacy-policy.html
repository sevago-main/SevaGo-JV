This Privacy Policy is published by SevaGo Technologies Pvt. Ltd. ("SevaGo", "we", "our", or "us") in accordance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

This policy governs two distinct contexts: (a) the sevago.co.in website (this page), and (b) the SevaGo mobile application available on Google Play. The scope of data collection differs materially between the two, and each is addressed separately below.

By accessing or using either the website or the application, you confirm that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and consent to the practices described herein.

Part A — Website (sevago.co.in)

A1. Data Collected by This Website

This website does not collect, store, or process any personal data from visitors. It is a static informational platform intended solely to describe the SevaGo application and facilitate its download. No cookies are set for tracking purposes, no analytics identifiers are stored, and no personal information is requested or retained by SevaGo through this website.

The sole exception is the job application form embedded in the "We're Hiring" section, which is hosted and operated by Google (Google Forms). Submissions made through that form are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. SevaGo receives application data you voluntarily submit through that form.

A2. Third-Party Services on This Website

This website may reference links to the Google Play Store and to SevaGo's Instagram profile. These are external platforms operated by third parties. SevaGo is not responsible for the privacy practices of Google, Meta, or any other linked service. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies before interacting with those platforms.

A3. AI-Powered Chat Assistant

This website includes an AI chat assistant ("SevaGo Assistant") powered by the Google Gemini API, provided by Google LLC. When you interact with this chat assistant, the messages you type are transmitted to Google's servers for processing and to generate a response.

What this means for you: Your chat messages are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate replies. SevaGo does not store your chat conversation on its own servers. The Gemini API is operated by Google, and your use of the chatbot is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

The chatbot is designed solely to answer questions about SevaGo. It does not collect personal data, ask for your name or contact details, and it has no access to any user account information. Do not share sensitive personal information (such as passwords, payment details, or ID numbers) through the chat.

By using the chat assistant, you acknowledge that your messages will be processed by Google's Gemini API in accordance with Google's data processing terms.

Part B — SevaGo Mobile Application

The following sections apply to users of the SevaGo mobile application, as published on Google Play by SevaGo Technologies Pvt. Ltd.

B1. Information Collected

Information you provide directly

  • Full name, email address, and mobile number upon account registration
  • Profile photograph (optional)
  • Government-issued identity documents. Collected from Taskers for identity verification only; permanently deleted within thirty (30) days of successful verification
  • Task descriptions, locations, and proposed budgets when posting or responding to tasks
  • In-app messages exchanged between users
  • Ratings and reviews submitted through the platform

Payment data

All payments are processed through a third-party payment gateway. SevaGo does not collect, receive, or store raw card numbers, UPI credentials, or net banking details. The payment provider's own privacy policy governs that data.

Data collected automatically

Consistent with our Play Store Data Safety declaration, the application automatically collects:

  • Location: Approximate location (from IP address at all times); precise GPS location only while the app is open and you have granted location permission
  • Device information: Device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers
  • App activity: Features accessed, session duration, and in-app navigation patterns
  • Diagnostic data: Crash reports and error logs to support platform stability

B2. Play Store Data Safety Summary

Data collected: Precise location and approximate location (while in use) · Name, email address, phone number · Profile photos · In-app messages · Task descriptions and reviews · App activity and interaction data · Device identifiers · Crash logs

Data shared: Your display name, profile photo (if provided), task history, and ratings are visible to the counterparty in a transaction. No data is shared with advertisers. No data is sold.

Data security: Data is encrypted in transit. You may request deletion of your data at any time.

B3. Purpose of Data Use

  • Account creation, authentication, and management
  • Proximity-based matching of Task Posters with Taskers
  • Payment processing and escrow management
  • Identity verification of Taskers prior to activation
  • Transactional notifications regarding task activity
  • Dispute investigation and resolution
  • Platform improvement based on aggregated, anonymised analytics
  • Fraud detection and prevention
  • Compliance with applicable Indian law

B4. Disclosure of Data

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. Disclosure occurs only as follows:

  • Between users: Display name, ratings, and task history are shared with counterparties in a transaction. Contact details are not shared unless you disclose them voluntarily.
  • Service providers: We engage third-party providers for cloud hosting, payment processing, and analytics under strict data processing agreements.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose data where required by law, court order, or competent regulatory authority.
  • Business transfer: In the event of a merger or acquisition, user data may transfer to the acquiring entity with prior notice to users.

B5. Data Retention

  • Account data: retained for the duration of the active account and for two (2) years post-deletion for legal and financial compliance
  • Task history, transactions, and reviews: retained for five (5) years
  • Identity documents: deleted within thirty (30) days of successful Tasker verification

B6. Your Rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, you have the right to access, rectify, delete, or obtain a portable copy of your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@sevago.co.in with the subject "Data Rights Request". We will respond within thirty (30) days.

To request account deletion, email hello@sevago.co.in with the subject "Delete My Account".

B7. Minors

The SevaGo application is intended for users who are eighteen (18) years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. Upon discovering that a minor has registered, we will promptly delete the account and associated data.

Changes to This Policy

We may amend this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via in-app notification or email prior to taking effect. Continued use of the platform constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
