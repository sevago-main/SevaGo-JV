SevaGo is a marketplace that connects people who post tasks (“Posters”) with verified people who do them (“Partners”). Contact: team@sevago.co.in.

2. The website (sevago.co.in)

The website is an informational page. It does not ask visitors for personal information, other than what you choose to send through the two features below, both of which involve a third party:

  • The hiring form is provided by a third-party form service. What you submit there is received by us and is also handled under that provider's privacy policy.
  • The chat assistant is provided by a third-party AI service. The messages you type are sent to that provider to produce a reply, and we do not store them on our own servers. Please do not type passwords, payment details or ID numbers into it. It cannot see any account.

The site also links to our app listing and to our pages on other platforms. Their privacy practices are theirs.

4. Who we share it with

  • The other person on your task: name, photo, rating and the task address (for the Partner), or the Partner's position while on the way (for the Poster). Phone numbers are not shown to each other.
  • Service providers that process data for us, such as providers of cloud hosting and storage, sign-in and SMS delivery, maps and address search, push notifications and crash reporting, and payments and payouts. They may use the data only to provide their service to us.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it or to protect someone's safety.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is open. If you delete your account we erase your personal information as described in Deleting your account, and keep only the records the law requires us to keep, without your name or phone number. Identity documents you give us for verification are deleted when your account is deleted.

6. Deleting your account

In the app

Open SevaGo or SevaGo Partner, go to Profile → Delete account, and confirm. You are signed out straight away and stop receiving notifications and tasks. Your account will be deleted in 10 days. If you sign in before then you will see “Deletion scheduled” and can tap Keep my account to cancel. After 10 days it cannot be undone. You can sign up again later with the same phone number as a new account.

If you cannot use the app

Email team@sevago.co.in with the subject “Delete my SevaGo account” and the mobile number registered on the account. We will check that the request is from you and then start the same 10-day period. We will tell you when it has started.

When we cannot delete yet

We cannot delete an account while you have a task in progress or open (including one under dispute), money owed to you, a payout on its way to your bank, or a refund still being processed. We will tell you which, and you can delete the account once it is finished.

What is deleted

After the 10 days: your name, phone number (replaced with a placeholder), profile photo, languages, saved addresses, notification devices and notification history; the messages and pictures you sent in chats; for Partners, verification documents and selfies (deleted from storage), date of birth, gender, home location, payout account details and daily goal; and our sign-in provider's record of your phone number.

7. Your rights

You can see and correct your profile in the app; delete your account in the app or by writing to us (section 6); withdraw a permission such as location in your phone's settings; ask for a copy of your information; and complain to our Grievance Officer (section 10). We reply as soon as we reasonably can. Write to team@sevago.co.in with the subject “Data Rights Request”.

8. Security

All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Verification documents are stored in private storage and shown only to our verification team through short-lived links, and every such access is recorded. No system is perfectly secure; if something affects you we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

9. Children

SevaGo is for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that a child has registered, we delete the account.
