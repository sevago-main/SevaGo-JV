Errand services in Coimbatore

Short on time? Post an errand on SevaGo and a local partner can pick it up, drop it off or handle it in person while you get on with your day.

SevaGo is live in Coimbatore and Bengaluru. Availability depends on your location and the partners available nearby, so some tasks may get offers faster than others.

Errands people post

  • Pick up or drop off parcels and personal items
  • Shopping pickups and small purchases
  • Collect documents from nearby locations
  • Local deliveries and drop-offs
  • Small tasks that need someone physically present

How to get a good result

  • Give exact pickup and drop locations, and the time you need it by
  • Describe the item (size, how many, anything fragile)
  • Say what proof you want, such as a photo on completion
  • Keep personal details out of the task description; use in-app messages for specifics

Budget guide

You set the budget and partners respond with offers. The minimum task budget in the app is ₹199, and any service fee is shown before you confirm. Budgets that match the distance, time and effort involved tend to attract better-matched offers.

How it works

  1. Post your task. Describe what you need done, add the location and your requirements.
  2. Find local help. Partners nearby can take up your request and send offers.
  3. Follow progress. Stay informed through task updates and in-app communication.
  4. Complete the task. Review the work and confirm completion. Your payment stays protected until you approve.

Staying safe

Every SevaGo Partner is KYC verified, and your payment is protected until you approve the work. Use in-app messages so task updates stay in one place.
