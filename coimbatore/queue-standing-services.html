Queue standing service in Coimbatore

Waiting in line costs hours. On SevaGo you can ask a local partner to wait in a queue or be present for an in-person task so you don’t have to.

SevaGo is live in Coimbatore and Bengaluru. Availability depends on your location and the partners available nearby, so some tasks may get offers faster than others.

When people use it

  • Waiting in a queue for a counter, collection or launch
  • Being present for an appointment, visit or follow-up
  • Document collection that needs someone in person
  • Arranging someone to be present for an everyday task

Before you post

  • Check that the place allows someone to act on your behalf for what you need
  • State the place, the time window and what the partner should do when they reach the front
  • Say if the partner should call or message you at a particular point

Budget guide

You set the budget and partners respond with offers. The minimum task budget in the app is ₹199, and any service fee is shown before you confirm. Budgets that match the distance, time and effort involved tend to attract better-matched offers. Longer waits usually mean more of the partner’s time, so budget accordingly.

How it works

  1. Post your task. Describe what you need done, add the location and your requirements.
  2. Find local help. Partners nearby can take up your request and send offers.
  3. Follow progress. Stay informed through task updates and in-app communication.
  4. Complete the task. Review the work and confirm completion. Your payment stays protected until you approve.

Frequently asked questions

Can a partner act on my behalf at any office?

Not necessarily. Rules differ by office and service. Check what the place allows before you post, and describe the task clearly.

How is payment handled?

Payment is made in the app and protected until you approve the work.

How long will the partner wait?

Set the time window in the task description and agree it through the in-app messages before the partner starts.
